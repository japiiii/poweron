Why solar in PNG

The sun doesn't send a bill.

Between grid outages, rising tariffs, and the cost of running generators, solar pays for itself — and PNG has some of the best sunlight in the world to do it with.

  • ✓

    Keep running through outages

    Battery-backed systems keep lights, fridges, tills, and equipment going when the grid drops out.

  • ✓

    Slash generator costs

    Fuel, servicing, and downtime add up fast. Solar cuts genset run-time dramatically — or replaces it entirely.

  • ✓

    Power remote sites

    Off-grid systems bring reliable power to locations the grid will never reach.

  • ✓

    Fixed, predictable costs

    Once installed, your power comes from sunlight. Your biggest cost is behind you, not ahead of you.
