Our Solutions

End-to-end energy solutions.

From a single household to an entire community — certified products of the highest quality, backed by warranties and our own technical team.

Solar Home Systems

Affordable, reliable solar power for individual households — lights, phone charging, and appliances, on or off the grid.

Institutional Power

Customised solar solutions for schools, hospitals, and government facilities where reliable power saves lives and enables learning.

SME & Tourism Energy

Renewable energy for trade stores, small businesses, lodges and tourism operators — cut costs and keep trading through outages.

Backup & Hybrid BESS

Battery energy storage systems combining solar, batteries and generators — 24/7 power through blackouts and unstable voltage.

Mini-Grids

Decentralised power grids that electrify entire remote communities — designed, permitted, installed and maintained by PowerOn.

How it works

From site visit to switch-on.

Our technicians handle the whole journey — pre-feasibility assessment, design, permits, installation, and maintenance.

STEP 01

Assessment

Pre-feasibility site assessment: we measure your load, check the site, and understand what you need to power.

STEP 02

Design & permits

Engineers design a compliant system for your load and budget, and we handle NEA permits and approvals.

STEP 03

Installation

Certified technicians install and commission the system to national regulations and international safety standards.

STEP 04

Support

After-sales service, maintenance, remote support, and community training — we stay with you long after switch-on.
